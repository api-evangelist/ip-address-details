IPinfo has launched ASN Activity Profiles, which display weekly heatmaps and behavioral metrics for autonomous systems networks. These profiles reveal distinct patterns: residential networks show day/night cycles, datacenters operate flatly around the clock, and office networks follow business hours, providing insights that static ownership records cannot.
