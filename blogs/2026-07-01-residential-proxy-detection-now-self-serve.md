IPinfo has made residential proxy detection available through self-serve access via IPinfo Max, starting at $78/month annually, eliminating the need for enterprise agreements. The company's dataset has grown 2.3x over the past year to over 107 million IPs, tracking connections that route traffic through consumer devices, commonly used in fraud, credential stuffing, and scraping attacks. The detection uses directly observed behavior rather than inference, providing users with signals like last-seen dates and consistency metrics to reduce false positives.
