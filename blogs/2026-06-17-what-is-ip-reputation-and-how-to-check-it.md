IP reputation measures the legitimacy and quality of an IP address, functioning similarly to a credit score. Poor reputation can impact website accessibility, ad performance, and transaction security by creating friction across business operations. The article explains how reputation is determined by factors like spam complaints and malware associations, and provides tools and strategies for monitoring and improving reputation scores.
