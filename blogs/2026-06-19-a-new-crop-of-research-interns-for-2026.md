IPinfo welcomed two research interns for summer 2026 as part of its second year running an internship program. Augustin, a PhD student from Ecole Normale Superieure de Lyon, is working on IPv6 address enumeration, while Santiago, a Northwestern University doctoral candidate, is investigating internet topology at the country level through network adjacency studies.
